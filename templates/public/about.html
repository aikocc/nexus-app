About Nexus Diagnostics

Bringing workshop-grade diagnostics to your driveway.

Founded by a team of automotive technicians and software engineers, Nexus Diagnostics was built on a simple premise: you shouldn't need to visit a workshop to know what's wrong with your car.

Our mobile diagnostic units are equipped with professional-grade OBD-II scanners, live data loggers, and inspection tools — everything a workshop has, packed into a service that comes to you.
